Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does Private Limited Company registration take?
+

The entire process typically takes 7 to 10 working days from when we receive all your documents — DSC issuance (1–2 days), name approval (1–3 days), and SPICe+ filing with MCA (2–4 days). For clients who provide documents promptly, we regularly complete registrations in 5–7 days.

Which ITR form should I file? What is the difference between ITR-1 and ITR-4?
+

ITR-1 (Sahaj) is for salaried individuals with income up to ₹50 lakh. ITR-2 is for those with capital gains or multiple properties. ITR-3 is for business/professional income (non-presumptive). ITR-4 (Sugam) is for presumptive taxation under Sections 44AD/44ADA — for small businesses and professionals. ITR-5 for firms & LLPs. ITR-6 for companies. We assess your profile and file the correct form.

Is PF registration mandatory? When must a business register for PF and ESI?
+

PF registration is mandatory for every establishment with 20 or more employees. ESI registration is mandatory for establishments with 10 or more employees where wages are ≤ ₹21,000/month. Both registrations must be completed before engaging employees. We handle registration and ongoing monthly ECR filing for you.

Can I register a company from a small town or village in Jharkhand?
+

Yes, absolutely. The entire process with Auditeo is 100% online. You can register from Ranchi, Hazaribagh, Giridih, Ramgarh, Bokaro, Dhanbad or any village in Jharkhand. All you need is your KYC documents and an address for the registered office — even a home address is acceptable.

What is your refund policy if registration fails?
+

We offer 100% refund of our professional fees if the registration fails within 30 days due to reasons within our control. Government fees and stamp duty paid to the government portal are non-refundable as these are paid directly to government authorities and cannot be reclaimed.

Do you provide GST return filing on a monthly retainer?
+

Yes. We offer monthly retainer packages for GST filing starting from ₹999/month (quarterly filers) and ₹1,499/month (monthly filers). This includes GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B preparation, ITC reconciliation, and annual GSTR-9 filing. You simply send us your sales and purchase data and we handle everything else completely.
