Overview

What is Income Tax Return (ITR) Filing?

Income Tax Return (ITR) is a form filed with the Income Tax Department of India reporting your income, deductions, and taxes paid during a financial year. Filing ITR is mandatory for individuals and entities whose income exceeds the basic exemption limit, or those who wish to claim a refund, carry forward losses, or apply for a loan or visa.

The due date for ITR filing is typically 31st July for individuals and 31st October for businesses requiring audit — for FY 2024-25 (AY 2025-26). Filing on time avoids penalties and interest under Sections 234A, 234B, and 234F.

📋 Governing Law

Income Tax Act, 1961 & Income Tax Rules, 1962. Administered by the Income Tax Department under CBDT.

📅 Due Dates

Individuals: 31 July. Businesses (non-audit): 31 July. Businesses (audit): 31 October. Companies: 31 October.

📈 Current FY

FY 2024-25 (AY 2025-26). New Tax Regime is now default. Old regime must be opted in explicitly.

⏳ Refund Timeline

IT refunds are typically processed within 15-45 days of e-verification. Auditeo tracks your refund status.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is required to file ITR? +
ITR filing is mandatory for: (1) Individuals with gross income exceeding the basic exemption limit (₹2.5L for <60 years, ₹3L for 60-80 years, ₹5L for 80+ years). (2) Companies and firms regardless of profit or loss. (3) Those who want to claim a refund or carry forward losses. (4) Persons with foreign assets or foreign income. (5) Those depositing ₹1 crore+ in bank, spending ₹2L+ on foreign travel, or paying ₹1L+ electricity bill.
What is the difference between Old Tax Regime and New Tax Regime? +
Old Regime: Higher tax rates but allows deductions like 80C (up to ₹1.5L), 80D (health insurance), HRA, home loan interest, etc. New Regime (default from FY 2024-25): Lower tax rates but most deductions are not available. New regime is now the default — you must explicitly opt for Old regime in your ITR if it is more beneficial. Our CAs will compute both and advise which saves you more tax.
What is the ITR filing due date for FY 2024-25? +
For FY 2024-25 (AY 2025-26): Individuals & HUFs (non-audit): 31 July 2025. Businesses requiring audit: 31 October 2025. Companies: 31 October 2025. Transfer pricing cases: 30 November 2025. Belated return: Up to 31 December 2025 with penalty. Updated ITR (ITR-U): Within 2 years from end of AY.
What is Form 26AS and AIS? Why is it important? +
Form 26AS is a consolidated tax statement showing TDS deducted by employers/banks, advance tax paid, self-assessment tax, and TCS. AIS (Annual Information Statement) is a comprehensive statement showing all financial transactions reported to the IT Department — including bank interest, mutual fund transactions, property purchases, and share trading. Before filing ITR, Auditeo always cross-verifies your income with 26AS and AIS to ensure accuracy and avoid notices.
What happens if I file ITR after the due date? +
Filing after the due date (but by 31 Dec): ₹1,000 penalty if income ≤₹5 lakh; ₹5,000 penalty if income >₹5 lakh under Section 234F. Additionally, you cannot carry forward losses (except house property loss), and interest under 234A/234B/234C continues to accrue on unpaid tax. We recommend filing on time to avoid these consequences.
I received an income tax notice — what should I do? +
Do not ignore any IT notice. Common notices are: 143(1) — intimation on return processing, 139(9) — defective return, 148 — income escaped assessment, 143(2) — scrutiny. Each type requires a specific response within the stipulated time. Auditeo’s CA team handles all types of IT notices — we analyse the notice, prepare the response, and file it on the income tax portal on your behalf.
Can I revise my ITR after filing? +
Yes. A revised return can be filed under Section 139(5) to correct mistakes in the original return. It can be filed any number of times before the due date of the belated return (31 December of AY) or before the IT Department completes the assessment, whichever is earlier. There is no penalty for filing a revised return. Auditeo can help you file a revised return if you notice any error.
I am an NRI — do I need to file ITR in India? +
NRIs must file ITR in India if: (1) Total Indian income exceeds basic exemption limit (₹2.5L). (2) They have capital gains from sale of Indian property or shares. (3) They have rental income from Indian property. (4) They wish to claim a TDS refund. NRI ITR filing (ITR-2) requires special attention to DTAA (Double Taxation Avoidance Agreement) provisions, FEMA compliance, and foreign tax credit claims. Auditeo has experience handling NRI returns.
